# How to update your website

Bassem, you never need to touch code. Everything on the site (texts, artworks, workshops, prices) is edited in a simple form, like filling in a document.

## Change anything on the site

1. Go to **https://app.pagescms.org** and click **Sign in with GitHub**.
2. Open the project **bassem-boules**, then **My website**.
3. Change what you want:
   - **Artworks**: click **+ Add** to add a new work, upload the photo, write the title, size, status and story. Drag works up or down to change the order. The first ones appear first.
   - **Workshops & teaching**: add your new workshop at the top of the list.
   - **About me**, **Commissions**, **Contact**: just edit the text.
4. Click **Save**. The live website updates by itself in about one minute.

Tips:
- Photos: use a clear, straight photo of the work, at least 1600 pixels wide. JPG is best.
- To start a new paragraph, leave one empty line.
- Made a mistake? Every save is kept, so your helper can always bring back an older version.

## The free guide (email sign-up)

Visitors who leave their email get the guide "The Old Masters' Portrait Notes" (the page `guide.html`).
To receive their emails in your inbox, your helper connects a free Formspree form once and pastes its address into **Free guide → Email service address**. Until then, visitors still get the guide, but emails are not saved anywhere.

## Costs

- Website hosting: free (GitHub Pages).
- Editor: free (Pages CMS).
- Email sign-ups: free up to 50 a month (Formspree).
- Your own address like bassemboules.com: optional, about 10–15 USD per year.

# طريقة تعديل موقعك

يا باسم، مش محتاج تلمس أي كود. كل حاجة في الموقع (النصوص، الأعمال، الورش، الأسعار) بتتعدل من فورم بسيط.

1. ادخل على **https://app.pagescms.org** واضغط **Sign in with GitHub**.
2. افتح مشروع **bassem-boules** وبعدها **My website**.
3. عدّل اللي انت عايزه:
   - **Artworks**: اضغط **+ Add** لإضافة عمل جديد، ارفع الصورة واكتب الاسم والمقاس والحالة والقصة. تقدر تسحب الأعمال لفوق أو لتحت لتغيير الترتيب.
   - **Workshops & teaching**: ضيف الورشة الجديدة في أول القائمة.
4. اضغط **Save**، والموقع هيتحدث لوحده خلال دقيقة تقريبًا.
